“We stopped asking what's trending, and started asking what we'd still want to wear in five years.” — Notes from the PERENNE design studio

2×collections per year
38pieces in the current range
10yrrepair promise on every piece

PERENNE started as a reaction to a wardrobe full of clothes worn twice. We now release two small collections a year instead of twelve, and every piece has to pass one test before it's cut: would we still want it in five years, not five weeks.

That means fewer prints, fewer logos, and a lot more attention paid to things you can't see on a hanger — the weight of a fabric, the give in a seam, the way a hem sits after the twentieth wash.

When something wears out, we'll repair it free of charge for as long as we're making clothes. That's not a marketing line; it's in the return label sewn into every garment.
